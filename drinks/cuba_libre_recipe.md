# Cuba Libre

A Cuba Libre is another name for a Rum and Coke with a slice of lemon or lime, and a squeeze of lemon or lime juice.

## Requisites

* **Coke**

* **Rum:** Dark or light, though dark is preferred.

* **Lemon juice or lime juice**

* *Optional:* A piece of lemon, lime, or orange to garnish.

## How to Make a Cuba Libre

1. Mix 25% rum and 75% Coke.

2. Add the lemon or lime juice to taste.

3. Garnish with your citrus slice and serve.
