A screwdriver is a simple cocktail made from vodka and orange juice. 

REQUISITES
  - Your favorite vodka
  - Your favorite orange juice
  - Optionally Some cinnamon, sugar, and/or nutmeg to sprinkle on top
  - Optionally a slice of orange to garnish on top

HOW TO MAKE A SCREWDRIVER 
Pour the ice in, then the vodka to 1/4 of the glass, then finish with the orange juice. Finally add whatever toppings you've prepared.

BLENDED VARIANT 
For the blended screwdriver variant, we either freeze the orange juice, or use some can of frozen orange juice. Then we just blend it.
