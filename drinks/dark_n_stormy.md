The Dark n' Stormy is another Carribean classic cocktail. In this cocktail, the hard punch of the rum is smoothed out by the thirst-quenching ginger beer and the zest of fresh lime juice. Generally, the Dark n' Stormy is served on the rocks. When done correctly, this cocktail really does look kind of like a tropical storm.

REQUISITES
  -Ginger Beer (local Carribean preferred for authentic Carribean flavour)
  -Dark Rum (local Carribean brands such as Cruzan preferred for authentic Carribean flavour)
  -Fresh Lime Juice (can substitute Lemon Juice if you're out of Lime Juice, or even use both lemon and lime if you wish)

HOW TO MAKE A DARK N STORMY
  Put ice in the cup, then pour the soda until it's 3/4 of the way full. Then, finish with the rum and lime juice on top. The top of the drink should be darker than the bottom. Normally, rum is poured before the soda, but with a dark n' stormy, it drinks and looks better if the rum is placed on top. This version of Dark N' Stormy gets less alcoholic and more refreshing with each sip you take.
