A Cuba Libre is another name for a Rum n Coke with a slice of lemon or lime, and a squeeze of lemon or lime juice.

REQUISITES  
  -Coke
  -Rum (dark or light but preferably dark)
  -lemon juice or lime juice
  -[optional] a piece of lemon, lime, or orange to garnish

  HOW TO MAKE A CUBA LIBRE  
  Mix 25% Rum and 75% coke, then add the juice to taste. Garnish.
