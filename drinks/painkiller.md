The Painkiller is a tropical island drink from the Virgin Islands in the Carribean. This dark-rum based drink is usually served on the rocks.

REQUISITES:
  - 1/4 Dark Rum, Cruzan Dark or other Carribean brand for authentic Carribean flavour
  - 1/4 Coco-Lopez or other coconut cream; you may substitute cream liqeur in a pinch
  - 1/4 Pineapple Juice
  - 1/4 Orange Juice
  - a little nutmeg
  - [optional] small orange/pineapple slice

PROCESS:
Combine every liquid in the glass/cup on the rocks. Add nutmeg on top, and garnish with a slice of citrus on the edge of the glass.

BLENDED VERSION:
Pre-freeze all liquids to desired crystallization (soft snow-like consistency recommended for non-rum ingredients), then combine the liquids in a blender, and finally add nutmeg on top and garnish.
