Get a perked-up feeling from this delicious combination of coffee and vodka. The Espresso Martini is versatile, so don't be scared of subsitutions (see footnotes). 

  REQUISITES:  
  -1/4 vodka  
  -1/4 coffee liqeur such as Kahlua  
  -1/4 cream (eg half n half), or cream liquer (such as Cape Fear Beach Blast), or milk, or evaporated milk, or even kefir  
  -1/4 espresso or black coffee  
  -[optional] some topping such as shaved chocolate, sugar, M&M's, marshmallows, or whatever you like to put in your coffee  
  
  HOW TO MAKE AN ESPRESSO MARTINI:  
  Mix all liquids in old-fashioned glass with no ice (neat), then add toppings. If you wish to chill the drink, put it in a chiller or a freezer. Or, drop it in ice. Well, I'm sure you can figure out how to make it cold, but this drink can actually be served warm as well. Perfect for those winter hikes. If you're in a rush and have no other way to chill the drink, you could serve this on the rocks, although being served on the rocks reduces the drink's potency.   

  SUBSTITUTION NOTES:  
  -Removing the cream / cream liqeur / white ingredient makes an Espresso Martini into a Black Espresso Martini, and pouring only 1/4 vodka and 3/4 Kahlua is a classic Black Russian. A Black Russian is essentially the same drink as a Black Espresso Martini, except with more alcohol content because it uses alcoholic coffee liqueur instead of non-alcoholic black coffee.  
  -Removing the cream and adding some lemon juice turns it into a Black Magic cocktail, which is a less alcoholic and more perky version of the Black Russian, with a zing of lemon juice to give it a nice kick
