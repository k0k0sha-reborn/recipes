# Rice Kasha (aka Rice Porridge, aka Arroz con Leche)

## Ingredients
* Rice
* Milk
* Water
* Butter
* Salt 
* Sugar (cane or refined)
* *Optional:* Vanilla flavoring, cinnamon, and/or raisins

## Instructions
1. Wash the rice first, until the water that touches the rice is clear. 
2. Boil the rice to an *al-dente* consistency in salted water. 
3. Drain the water, and put in as much milk as you had water in the pot. The milk should preferably be warm, but you may use cold milk. 
4. Add about a tablespoon of butter per pot. 
5. Add about 3 tablespoons of sugar. You may add the sugar later, or you may do it as soon as you mix the milk in. 
   > **Note:** Do not add too much butter or sugar, and ensure the taste is still agreeable after every spoon of sugar. This dish is very easy to over-sweeten.
6. If you have vanilla flavoring, you may mix a very small amount into the kasha at this point. Do not put too much in.
7. Once the milk is in the pot, put it at a low heat and cook it for 45 minutes to 1 hour, stirring often. 
8. Finally, mix in the raisins, cinnamon, and any other berries you're having in it. 
9. Serve alongside a protein such as ham or bologna.

Note that while this recipe can be made as a dessert, it can also be eaten as an entire meal. It is a common breakfast in certain parts of the world.
