TUSCAN OIL SPREAD
A wonderful dipping sauce from Italy, Tuscan Oil is served alongside bread or pretzels. Although the flavour is considered Italian, this dish is found in other European cuisines. This is a simple dish with only a few ingredients that can be prepared in minutes. In selecting the ingredients for this dish, try to select quality ingredients, rather than going for the cheapest thing on the shelf. The bread should be fresh, and the oil, cheese, and vinegar need to be gourmet quality. If you're going to make this dish, you should make it with fine ingredients. With that said, none of the ingredients are super expensive; just don't use the cheapest stuff on the shelf. I like sourcing my ingredients from Italy, France, Spain, and Portugal. 

INGREDIENTS
-Fresh bread, breadsticks, or pretzels (you'll be dipping them as you eat)
-Quality Olive Oil (try to avoid the cheap stuff) (also if Olive Oil isn't available, you may substitute Pomace oil, or use a blend of Pomace and Olive Oil)
-Quality Balsamic Vinegar
-Quality Parmesan Cheese, grated or powdered. I recommend Parmiggiano Reggiano from Italiy
-Garlic, minced into small pieces
-Optionally, some fresh ground herbs and ground pink peppercorn as a sprinkling on top

DIRECTIONS
Step 1. Ensure your cheese is grated and your garlic is minced into small pieces. The garlic and cheese make up the base of the dish. A quality Parmesan cheese has a strong flavour, and it will therefore dominate the other flavours. This is fine. If you don't want the cheese to overwhelm the dish, you may either choose a cheese with a less powerful flavour, or you may use less cheese.
Step 2. Take a little saucer plate, which you'll be serving the Tuscan oil in. Rub the minced garlic all over the plate with a spoon until the plate is covered with a thin layer of garlic juice. Remove the excess garlic-- you may use it later for other purposes by putting it back, if you want.
Step 3. Take the Parmesan cheese and put it in the center of the dish, in a little pile. You may also just scatter it around the plate in an even layer.
Step 4. Fill the saucer most of the way with the olive oil
Step 5. Add a ring of Balsamic Vinegar into the olive oil, around the pile of cheese. You don't need too much Balsamic; a couple tablespoons suffices. You may adjust this to taste.
Step 6. If you wish, add a pinch of the ground herbs and the ground peppercorns as a topping
Step 7. Serve with bread.
