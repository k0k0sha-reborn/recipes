# Tuscan Oil Spread

A wonderful dipping sauce from Italy, Tuscan Oil is served alongside bread or pretzels. Although the flavour is considered Italian, this dish is found in other European cuisines. This is a simple dish with only a few ingredients that can be prepared in minutes.

> **Ingredient Quality Note:** In selecting the ingredients for this dish, try to choose quality items rather than going for the cheapest thing on the shelf. The bread should be fresh, and the oil, cheese, and vinegar need to be gourmet quality. None of the ingredients are super expensive—just avoid the cheapest options. I like sourcing my ingredients from Italy, France, Spain, and Portugal.

## Ingredients

* **Fresh Bread, Breadsticks, or Pretzels:** For dipping as you eat.
* **Quality Olive Oil:** Try to avoid the cheap stuff. *(Note: If olive oil isn't available, you may substitute pomace oil, or use a blend of pomace and olive oil.)*
* **Quality Balsamic Vinegar**
* **Quality Parmesan Cheese:** Grated or powdered. I recommend *Parmigiano Reggiano* from Italy.
* **Garlic:** Minced into small pieces.
* *Optional:* Fresh ground herbs and ground pink peppercorn as a sprinkling on top.

## Directions

1. **Prep the base:** Ensure your cheese is grated and your garlic is minced into small pieces. The garlic and cheese make up the base of the dish. 
   * *Tip:* A quality Parmesan cheese has a strong flavour and will dominate the other flavours, which is fine. If you don't want the cheese to overwhelm the dish, you may either choose a cheese with a less powerful flavour or use less cheese.
2. **Season the plate:** Take a little saucer plate (which you'll be serving the Tuscan oil in) and rub the minced garlic all over the plate with a spoon until it is covered with a thin layer of garlic juice. Remove the excess garlic—you can put it back to use later for other purposes if you want.
3. **Add the cheese:** Take the Parmesan cheese and put it in the center of the dish in a little pile. Alternatively, you may scatter it around the plate in an even layer.
4. **Pour the oil:** Fill the saucer most of the way with the olive oil.
5. **Add the vinegar:** Pour a ring of balsamic vinegar into the olive oil around the pile of cheese. You don't need too much balsamic—a couple of tablespoons suffices, though you may adjust this to taste.
6. **Garnish (Optional):** Add a pinch of the ground herbs and ground peppercorns as a topping, if desired.
7. **Serve** immediately with your fresh bread.
